# Current at UBC

> Student-to-industry events platform connecting UBC students with senior leaders in finance, banking, venture capital, and tech.

🌐 **Live site:** [currentatubc.com](https://currentatubc.com)

---

## About

Current at UBC is a co-founded events platform launching **October 21, 2026** with our flagship event:

- **350+ UBC students**
- **3 keynote speakers**
- **50 Vancouver industry leaders** across finance, banking, VC, and tech
- Partnered with **BC Children's Hospital Foundation** (net proceeds donated)

The thesis: nepotism dominates entry-level hiring, especially in the AI era. Most students who don't come from connected families have no real way to meet the people doing the hiring. We're building the bridge.

The platform is designed to scale to other Canadian universities after the UBC launch.

---

## What's in this repo

The production website + full automation backend powering registration, sponsor outreach, ticketing, and partner workflows.

**Stack:**

- **Frontend:** React, deployed on Netlify
- **DNS / Email:** Cloudflare with custom domain
- **Automation:** Zapier pipelines wiring form submissions through Google Sheets into Slack alerts and a custom CRM
- **Sponsor Portal:** Tier logic for sponsor packages and partner intake

---

## Features

- Public-facing event registration with form submission pipeline
- Real-time Slack notifications to founders for new sign-ups, sponsor inquiries, and speaker confirmations
- Automated CRM sync for stakeholder tracking
- Sponsor tier portal with pricing and benefit logic
- Custom email infrastructure on Cloudflare
